Grow one farm. Belong to a whole valley.

Sprout Hollow Valley is a local-first farming life simulation in one expanded, authored open world. Choose one of eight estates, work the land, enter every structure, and build lasting relationships with a persistent community.

The complete-release contract

One authored valley, built for a long life

The target is a connected third-person world with deterministic farming, production, community life, and recoverable consequences. It is not an endless generated map and it never depends on online-generated dialogue.

World

Eight starting estates

Meadow, forest, riverland, mountain, coastal, marsh, arid, and alpine estates share one expanded valley.

Simulation

Deterministic by design

Seasons, weather, markets, production, schedules, and life events resolve from saved state rather than the wall clock.

Community

Exactly 240 named NPCs

Every resident has a home, work, relationships, routines, memories, and authored local conversation coverage.

Boundaries

A complete local package

Models, animation, textures, materials, audio, rules, and dialogue ship with the application; play never requires an account.

All 700 structures are fully enterable

No painted doors and no empty façades

Every factory and non-factory building has a usable entrance, streamed interior, complete room graph, and a real destination for every visible door. A locked door must explain why and provide an eventual access path.

  • Purposeful rooms. Homes, shops, workplaces, civic buildings, farms, mines, and venues include the secondary rooms their work and residents need.
  • Functional stations. Intake, storage, preparation, production, quality, shipping, maintenance, safety, and service stations have real interactions and saved state.
  • Sanitation everywhere. Every structure includes an accessible restroom and hand-washing route appropriate to its size and use.
  • People use the space. Staffing, opening hours, visitor behaviour, schedules, storage, utilities, and navigation make interiors part of the simulation.

Independent from Sprout Hollow

A separate application, save, and update path

Sprout Hollow Valley preserves the original project's MIT attribution while using its own application ID, user-data directory, fresh save format, executable identity, update feed, repository, and release history. Original installs and saves stay untouched.

Local by default

No online account, cloud save, telemetry, analytics, advertising, paid content, or microtransactions are part of the first complete release.

Bundled assets

The game does not fetch models, textures, audio, animation, or dialogue while you play. Release checks use GitHub only when you ask the updater.

This page stays modest

No trackers, analytics, advertising, cookies, or external fonts. The page stores only your language choice and contacts GitHub to enhance the static release link.
